# ============================================================================
# FROZEN BASELINE PROGRAM -- PATCHED (E1 repair, C1 collision counterexample).
# ============================================================================
# Observed failure: a rolling entry (branch D) fired while an oncoming vehicle
# was stopped at the mouth of the conflict zone. A stationary oncoming car has
# an effectively infinite time-to-conflict-zone, so OncomingGapExceeds passed;
# branch C then re-committed past the stop line with no clearance test at all,
# and the ego drove through the junction at 6.5 m/s and clipped the stopped car.
#
# Repair, three localised edits, structure otherwise untouched:
#   * C now carries a PathIsClear guard (it was the only commit branch with
#     none); if it fails the existing E/F branches settle the ego down instead.
#   * D's clearance test lengthened and its entry speed reduced, so a rolling
#     entry needs a genuinely empty turn path.
#   * A keeps its "never abandon the turn" priority, but no longer drives into
#     a stationary obstacle: a short-range clearance test picks between
#     committing and crawling. The range is deliberately small (6 m) so this is
#     a last-resort obstacle guard, not a mid-junction yield.
# ============================================================================

# Unprotected left turn at a signalised four-way junction.
# Priority order: finish what you started > decide from a stop >
# finish a rolling entry > enter on green with a real gap > stop at the line >
# back off behind the lead > cruise the approach.

Selector

  # --- A. Already inside the conflict zone: never abandon the turn. ---
  Sequence
    InConflictZone
    Selector
      # Keep driving through unless something is physically right in front.
      Sequence
        PathIsClear(dist_m=6.0)
        CommitTurn(v_mps=5.5, accel_mps2=2.0)
      # Last resort: an obstacle is a few metres away on the path -- crawl,
      # do not drive into it. Re-commits the instant the path opens again.
      Decelerate(v_mps=0.5, decel_mps2=2.5)

  # --- B. Stopped at (or just over) the stop line: this is the decision point. ---
  Sequence
    EgoSpeedBelow(v_mps=1.0)
    DistanceToStopLineBelow(dist_m=4.0)
    Selector
      # Normal go: green, a gap well beyond the 3.0 s contract, path physically clear.
      Sequence
        TrafficLightIsGreen
        OncomingGapExceeds(gap_s=5.5)
        PathIsClear(dist_m=16.0)
        CommitTurn(v_mps=6.5, accel_mps2=2.0)
      # Deadlock escape: nobody ever gives a big gap, so take a smaller but still legal one.
      Sequence
        TrafficLightIsGreen
        WaitedLongerThan(t_s=12.0)
        OncomingGapExceeds(gap_s=3.8)
        PathIsClear(dist_m=14.0)
        CommitTurn(v_mps=6.0, accel_mps2=2.0)
      # Otherwise sit still, gently.
      HoldAtStopLine(decel_mps2=2.0)

  # --- C. Past the stop line and still rolling: committed, drive through. ---
  # (The stopped-past-the-line case is caught by B above, so this only fires while moving.)
  Sequence
    DistanceToStopLineBelow(dist_m=0.0)
    PathIsClear(dist_m=12.0)
    CommitTurn(v_mps=5.5, accel_mps2=2.0)

  # --- D. Rolling entry: green, generous gap, clear path, close to the line. ---
  Sequence
    TrafficLightIsGreen
    DistanceToStopLineBelow(dist_m=14.0)
    OncomingGapExceeds(gap_s=6.5)
    PathIsClear(dist_m=24.0)
    CommitTurn(v_mps=6.0, accel_mps2=2.0)

  # --- E. Near the junction, road ahead clear: settle onto the stop line and wait. ---
  Sequence
    DistanceToStopLineBelow(dist_m=18.0)
    PathIsClear(dist_m=20.0)
    HoldAtStopLine(decel_mps2=2.0)

  # --- F. Near the junction but something (lead or crossing traffic) is in the way. ---
  Sequence
    DistanceToStopLineBelow(dist_m=30.0)
    Decelerate(v_mps=1.0, decel_mps2=2.0)

  # --- G. Open approach: cruise, but keep an RSS-safe buffer behind the lead. ---
  Selector
    Sequence
      PathIsClear(dist_m=28.0)
      Cruise(v_mps=7.0, accel_mps2=1.5)
    Decelerate(v_mps=4.0, decel_mps2=1.5)
